Privacy notice

Version 2026-10-07. Written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What Pathya stores

  • Your account: email address, a scrambled (hashed) password, a scrambled backup key, and when you gave consent.
  • Your health details: profile, conditions, medicines, readings, meals, chat with Pathya, documents and photos you upload, care-team contacts and escalations.

We don't collect location, contacts, advertising IDs or analytics, and we don't sell or share data with anyone.

Why

Only to give you personalised food guidance and to keep your data in sync across your phones. Pathya's chat runs inside the app; your questions are not sent to any AI service.

Where and how it's kept

  • On Microsoft Azure servers in the Central India (Pune) region.
  • Health details and files are encrypted before they're saved. Connections use HTTPS.
  • A copy stays in your phone's browser storage so the app works offline. Logging out removes it from that phone.

Who sees it

Only you. Data leaves your account only when you print, share on WhatsApp, copy, or export it.

Your rights

  • See and download everything: Profile → Account → Download my data.
  • Correct anything in the app at any time.
  • Withdraw consent and erase: Profile → Account → Delete my account. Your data is removed from our servers immediately.
  • If your account is unused for 2 years, we delete it.

Age

Accounts are for people aged 18 or over. A parent or guardian can create an account to manage a child's diet.

Medical safety

Pathya gives food guidance, not medical advice. It never changes medicines. In an emergency call 112.

Questions or complaints

Contact the Pathya team (grievance contact to be added before public launch). You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
