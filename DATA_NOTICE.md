# Data notice

`data/pathya-data.js` is generated by `scripts/build_data.py` from:

- **Pathya nutrition database** (`pathya_food.db`). Dish and food nutrient values are derived from the *Indian Food Composition Tables 2017* (ICMR-NIN) and the *Indian Nutrient Databank* (INDB), with a few USDA analogues for staples.
- **Pathya dietitian kit**: pathways, medicine–nutrient rules, screeners, education cards and fasting rules, with their cited sources.

The upstream data card marks the IFCT/INDB-derived content as **for personal use only**. Reproducing it in an electronic product requires permission from the rights holders. Before distributing this app or hosting it publicly, either obtain that permission or replace `data/pathya-data.js` with data you are licensed to publish.

Clinical rules are simplified for food guidance. They are not medical advice and do not replace a doctor or registered dietitian.
